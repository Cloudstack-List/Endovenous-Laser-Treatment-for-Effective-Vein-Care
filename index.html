Endovenous Laser Treatment for Effective Vein Care

Varicose veins are more than just a cosmetic concern. They can cause aching, swelling, fatigue, and discomfort in your legs. Endovenous laser treatment (EVLT) offers a minimally invasive, effective solution to treat varicose veins while allowing you to resume daily life quickly. At Sheen Vein (Aesthetics and Functional Medicine) in St. Louis, patients receive expert care that addresses both appearance and vein health.

Whether you’re seeking relief from pain or want smoother, healthier-looking legs, understanding how EVLT works and what to expect can help you make an informed decision.

What Is Endovenous Laser Treatment (EVLT)?

EVLT is a minimally invasive procedure used to treat varicose veins by closing off the affected veins with laser energy. The procedure redirects blood flow to healthier veins, improving circulation and relieving symptoms like swelling, heaviness, and leg fatigue.

EVLT is widely regarded as the gold standard in modern vein care. It has largely replaced traditional vein-stripping surgery because it is effective, low-risk, and leaves virtually no scarring. Unlike older procedures, EVLT is performed through a small incision, allowing for a quicker recovery and less discomfort.

A qualified vein specialist evaluates each patient individually to determine if EVLT is the right solution. A personalized approach ensures safety and optimal results.

How EVLT Works

During EVLT, a thin laser fiber is carefully inserted into the damaged vein through a small incision. The laser emits targeted energy that heats the vein wall, causing it to collapse and seal shut. This process stops the backward flow of blood and reduces the appearance and discomfort of varicose veins.

Once the treated vein is closed, surrounding healthy veins take over the role of returning blood to the heart. The procedure is minimally invasive, usually completed on an outpatient basis, and allows most patients to resume normal activities quickly.

Benefits of EVLT Over Traditional Surgery

EVLT offers several advantages compared to traditional vein-stripping surgery:

  • Minimally Invasive: Tiny incisions result in minimal scarring.
  • Quick Recovery: Patients can usually resume normal activities shortly after the procedure.
  • Pain Reduction: The procedure is relatively painless with only minor discomfort afterward.
  • Long-Lasting Results: Treated veins remain closed, providing durable relief.
  • Improved Appearance: Varicose veins are reduced, leaving the treated area smoother and healthier-looking.

EVLT works by addressing the underlying vein problem rather than just removing visible veins. By closing the damaged vein and redirecting blood flow to healthy veins, it provides a lasting solution to varicose veins, consistent with the process described on the website.

Who Is a Candidate for EVLT?

EVLT may be suitable if you experience:

  • Bulging, twisted, or visible varicose veins
  • Chronic leg pain, aching, or cramping
  • Swelling or heaviness, particularly after standing for long periods
  • Skin discoloration or vein-related inflammation

A vein specialist will typically perform a detailed ultrasound examination to assess your veins and determine if EVLT is the right option. This allows for a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.

Considerations Before EVLT

Before undergoing EVLT, it’s important to:

  • Discuss potential risks and benefits with your specialist
  • Ask questions about recovery timelines and lifestyle restrictions
  • Review any medications that may affect healing
  • Understand insurance coverage and payment options

A personalized consultation ensures patients are well-informed and confident about the treatment.

Recovery After EVLT

Recovery from EVLT is generally quick and straightforward:

  • Walking Immediately: Patients are encouraged to walk after the procedure to promote circulation.
  • Compression Garments: Worn for several days to help reduce swelling and support healing.
  • Bruising or Soreness: Mild bruising or tenderness is normal and typically resolves naturally.
  • Activity: Most daily activities can be resumed soon after the procedure, though heavy exercise may be limited for a short period.

The minimal downtime compared to traditional surgery allows patients to continue their routines with little disruption.

EVLT vs Other Vein Treatments

EVLT is a minimally invasive option that provides effective relief from varicose veins with little discomfort and minimal scarring.

Traditional vein-stripping surgery is more invasive, involves a longer recovery period, and may leave more noticeable scars. Sclerotherapy is another minimally invasive approach, but it is generally best suited for smaller spider veins rather than larger varicose veins.

EVLT is particularly effective for treating larger veins and saphenous reflux, addressing the underlying vein problem, while sclerotherapy is typically reserved for cosmetic improvement of smaller veins.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is EVLT painful?
The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, keeping it comfortable. Mild soreness after treatment is normal and typically resolves on its own.

What is the recovery like?
Most patients can return to their normal daily activities shortly after the procedure. Walking is encouraged immediately to support circulation, and any mild bruising or tenderness usually improves quickly.

Are the results permanent?
Treated veins are closed and blood flow is redirected to healthy veins. This provides lasting relief from varicose vein symptoms.

Do I need to wear compression garments?
Wearing compression garments for several days after treatment helps reduce swelling and supports the healing process.

How do I know if EVLT is right for me?
A vein specialist can evaluate your condition using a detailed ultrasound assessment to determine whether EVLT is safe and suitable for your needs.
